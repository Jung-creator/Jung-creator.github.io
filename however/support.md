# Support

**However** — an iPhone app for practicing English phrasal verbs. You write a sentence, an
on-device AI writes the next one, and the story goes wherever the wind leads.

## Contact

Questions, feedback, or something not working? Email
[junchoia25@gmail.com](mailto:junchoia25@gmail.com) and you will get a reply.

When you report a problem, please include:

- Your device model
- Your iOS version
- What happened

## Frequently asked questions

### Does However require an internet connection?

No. However does not require an internet connection to use its core features.

The AI runs on-device, and the sentences you write are stored directly on your device. Your data
does not need to be uploaded to a server to create and continue your story.

### Where are my sentences stored?

The sentences you write are stored only on your device.

They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers. Your writing stays on your device and is not
accessible to us.

### What happens if I delete the app?

If you delete However, the sentences you have written and other data stored on your device will
also be deleted.

There is currently no way to recover your writing after the app has been deleted. We recommend
keeping the app installed if you want to keep your writing.
