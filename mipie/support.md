# Support

**MiPie** — an iPhone app that quietly maps the Wi-Fi networks of the places where you settle in
to work. Cafés, libraries, community centers. Each record draws a 50 m circle, meaning "somewhere
in here, I used this Wi-Fi."

## Why nothing was recorded

MiPie records **only some** of the networks you join, on purpose. If it stayed silent, one of these
is usually why.

| What you see | Why |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens when you open the app | The network is already recorded. Each network is saved once, and after that the app stays quiet |
| "Open Wi-Fi networks aren't recorded" | The network needs no password or login. Networks you join automatically on the street or on transit would otherwise fill the map |
| "Not recorded while you're moving" | You are moving, or your device's recent activity says you were in a vehicle within the last few minutes — including a bus stopped at a light |
| "Personal hotspots aren't recorded" | A hotspot follows a person, not a place |
| "Getting a precise location…" | The location fix is off by more than 100 m. The app draws a 50 m circle, so a worse fix would make that circle a lie. Common underground and on subways |
| "Connect to Wi-Fi first" | You are on cellular only |
| "Motion & Fitness permission needed" | Without activity history the app cannot tell a café from a bus, so it declines to record. Tap the button to open Settings |

**Two permissions are required.** Location must be *While Using the App* **with Precise Location
on** — iOS discloses a Wi-Fi network's name only to apps granted precise location, so the feature
cannot work without it. Motion & Fitness must be allowed, or no records are created at all.

**The app must be open.** iOS does not tell apps about Wi-Fi connections in the background, so a
record is created when you open MiPie while connected — not while it is closed. There is no way
around this.

## Removing a record

Tap a pin, then **Delete this record**. Deleting is the intended escape hatch — no filter is
perfect, and a place you did not mean to save can always be removed. Deleting the app removes
everything.
